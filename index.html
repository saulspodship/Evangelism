02 THE CONVERSATION

Begin with
listening.

People are not projects. A thoughtful question and an unhurried ear often open the door to an honest conversation.

  1. 01

    Ask permission. “Would you be open to talking about faith?”

  2. 02

    Listen before you answer. Let their story shape where you go next.

  3. 03

    Speak simply. Share hope without forcing a response.

“Always be prepared to give an answer ... but do this with gentleness and respect.” 1 Peter 3:15

04 WHEN QUESTIONS COME

Questions about
evangelism.

Faith-filled conversations can include honest doubt. Find clear, Scripture-rooted answers to common questions about sharing the gospel, Christian evangelism, respectful dialogue, and reaching Gen Z.

SHORT ANSWER

Christian evangelism is sharing the good news of Jesus with clarity, humility, and love—an invitation, never pressure.

Explore the theological archive
“I’m not religious. Where do I begin?”

Start with Jesus, not labels. Ask what they are curious about, then consider reading a short passage from John’s Gospel together. Don’t assume what they believe; let them tell you.

John 1:1–18
“What if I don’t know the answer?”

It’s okay to say, “I don’t know, but I’d like to explore that with you.” Honest humility builds trust. The aim is not to win a debate; it is to point toward Jesus faithfully.

James 1:19
“What if they say no?”

Respect their answer without pressure. Friendship is not a transaction. Continue to show care, and trust that the timing and outcome are not yours to control.

1 Peter 3:15
“How do I talk with someone of another faith?”

Begin with respect. Ask about their story, represent their beliefs fairly, and explain your hope without caricature or coercion. A generous conversation makes room for both people to be heard.

Colossians 4:5–6
“What is evangelism?”

Evangelism is sharing the good news of Jesus with clarity, humility, and love. It is an invitation—not pressure, manipulation, or a sales pitch.

Romans 1:16
“How do I share the gospel with someone?”

Ask permission, listen first, speak simply about Jesus and the hope you have received, then leave room for questions. Respect their freedom to respond.

Colossians 4:5–6
“How can I evangelize respectfully?”

Treat people as people rather than projects. Listen generously, represent others fairly, avoid coercion, and speak with gentleness and respect.

1 Peter 3:15
“How can I evangelize online or with Gen Z?”

Use short, honest stories and real conversation. Ask thoughtful questions, listen across digital spaces, and connect online conversations to friendship, Scripture, prayer, and community.

Colossians 4:5–6
