GUIDE 07 — PRACTICE

Tools & techniques
to engage Gen Z.

Three arenas—mission, church, and discipleship—and for each one, the tools and the techniques that go with them. Treat it as a menu: pick two things, and put a date on them.

  • AuthorTariq Emmanuel
  • ThemePractical Gen Z ministry
  • ReadingAbout 7 minutes
  • OriginalTools & Techniques handout (PDF)
HOW TO READ THIS GUIDE

This is the most practical handout in the library: three arenas—mission, church, and discipleship—and for each one, the tools and the techniques that go with them. Treat it as a menu, not a checklist. Pick two or three things and actually do them.

01

Tools and techniques for mission

Reaching outward

Mission — digital

A. Digital missions tools

Tools

  • Instagram Reels and TikTok gospel clips
  • WhatsApp evangelism groups
  • YouTube testimonies
  • Social media challenges, such as a 7-day gospel challenge
  • Online Bible study rooms (Zoom)

Techniques

  • Use short, visual, story-based content
  • Start “Ask Me Anything about God” sessions
  • Share testimonies in video format
  • Encourage youth to post their faith stories
  • Train them to use social media ethically and missionally
BIBLICAL MODEL

Paul in the marketplace (Acts 17:17). Today's marketplace is online platforms—same method, different square.

Mission — relationship

B. Relationship-based evangelism

Tools

  • Testimony nights
  • Coffee conversations
  • Conversation starter cards
  • Friendship evangelism training

Techniques

  • Teach them to share the gospel in one minute
  • Teach listening skills
  • Encourage them to start microgroups among peers
  • Use real-life stories instead of formulas
KEY PRINCIPLE

They follow Jesus through relationships, not programmes.

Mission — service

C. Service and justice missions

Tools

  • Community service projects
  • Food drives
  • Environmental clean-up
  • Helping poor families
  • Mission trips and urban outreach

Techniques

  • Combine service with Scripture reflection
  • Let youth lead the projects
  • Show how justice is part of God's mission (Micah 6:8)
  • End every outreach with prayer and testimony sharing
Acts 17:17 Micah 6:8
02

Tools and techniques for church

Making a room worth staying in

Church — culture

A. Create a “belong before believe” culture

Build it with

  • Small groups and home fellowships
  • Mentorship programmes
  • Youth-friendly welcome teams
  • Church social events
  • A warm, relational environment
  • Transparent leaders
  • Questions encouraged without judgement
  • Celebrating youth contributions

The principle

  • Jesus welcomed people before they believed (Mark 2:15–17).
  • Belonging is not a reward for believing; it is the doorway to it.
Church — worship

B. Creative and participatory worship

Tools

  • Youth-led worship teams
  • Spoken-word poetry
  • Testimony moments
  • Drama and skits
  • Interactive preaching

Techniques

  • Shorter, sharper sermons—8 to 12 minutes with discussion
  • Use multimedia during sermons
  • Invite youth to share Scripture, pray, or lead
Church — serving

C. Skill-based involvement

Tools

  • Media team training
  • Worship workshops
  • Camera, sound, and graphics training
  • Tech ministry
  • Social media team

Techniques

  • Give them real responsibilities
  • Assign weekly roles
  • Provide leadership training
PRINCIPLE

Equip the saints for ministry (Ephesians 4:11–12). A young person with a real role is not a risk to be managed; they are the plan.

Mark 2:15–17 Ephesians 4:11–12
03

Tools and techniques for discipleship

Growing in Christ

Discipleship — groups

A. Small-group discipleship

Tools

  • Three-to-five person discipleship groups
  • Bible reading plans
  • WhatsApp accountability chats
  • Weekly one-on-one mentoring

Techniques

  • Bible study in three steps: Observation → Interpretation → Application
  • Accountability questions
  • Prayer partnership
  • Scripture memorisation challenges
Discipleship — Scripture

B. Creative Bible engagement

Tools

  • Bible journaling
  • Verse-mapping
  • Scripture art
  • Dramatised Bible stories
  • Inductive study worksheets

Techniques

  • Use visuals and media in teaching
  • Let them teach a verse back to the group
  • Give practical weekly challenges
Discipleship — life on life

C. Mentorship and modelling

Tools

  • Mentoring pairing
  • Life-story sharing
  • Accountability partners
  • Regular life-on-life meetings

Techniques

  • Share your struggles and victories
  • Teach spiritual disciplines by modelling them
  • Help them identify their calling and spiritual gifts

Weekly challenges worth trying

  • Share Christ with one friend this week.
  • Pray for five minutes every day.
  • Read one Psalm a day.

These three are quoted directly from the handout. They are small on purpose—small enough that a young believer will actually finish them, and repeatable enough to become a habit.

}
04

Putting it together

Choose two, and put a date on them

The handout gives a wide menu. The danger is admiring it. Choose two things from this page and write them as goals this week.

  1. Pick one mission tool—probably the digital one, if your youth are already there.
  2. Pick one church or discipleship practice—something that changes a structure rather than an event.
  3. Write both into the goal planner as SMART goals with dates, and put the names of the people involved on your prayer wall.
  4. Ask one young person to lead one part of it. That is the whole method.
