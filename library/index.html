03 ABOUT THE LIBRARY

Attributed, adapted,
and honest about it.

These pages are web editions of other people's teaching. Credit stays with the authors, and every page links to the handout it came from.

HOW THESE PAGES WERE MADE

Each guide is a faithful summary and re-layout of an original seminar handout—the teaching, the Scripture references, and the practical steps belong to its author. Where a handout was written for a live session, the group activities are kept and adapted so one person can still do them alone.

Read the originals. Print them for your team. The Drive folder is the source, and this library is only a doorway to it.

Attribution first

Every guide names its author, and the header of each page links straight to the original PDF.

Scripture in full view

Verse references are kept as the author gave them, with links so you can read the passage yourself.

Offline by default

The whole library is precached, so it opens on a train, in a village, or in a room with no signal.

Private by default

Your goals and prayer notes never leave your device. There is no account and no server.

How to use it well

  1. Start where you are. A youth leader might begin with Key Characteristics of Gen Z; a pastor with Biblical Stewardship; a new believer with Communicating the Gospel.
  2. Read a guide with someone else. Most of these handouts were written for a room, not a reader. Read one aloud with your team and stop at the questions.
  3. Turn it into a plan. Take the guide that convicted you most and open the goal planner. Write it down while it is still warm.
  4. Carry the names. Put the people you are praying for on the prayer wall, and let the reading stay personal.
