A vision, a mission, seven areas of life, and an action plan with names attached. Built on Naeem Khokhar's seminar handout, and saved privately on this device as you type.

  • StorageThis device only — nothing is uploaded
  • Works offlineYes, once the library has loaded

PART ONE — DIRECTION

Where is God leading you?

Two statements do two different jobs. The vision is the long-term direction; the mission is how you will live it out day by day.

PART TWO — GOALS

SMART goals across seven areas of life

Specific, measurable, action-oriented, realistic, and time-bound. Write at least one goal in each area that helps you live out your mission. Tick the box when it is finished.

A. Ministry

Where will you make the gospel known?

Example from the handout: Lead a Bible study group for young adults every Wednesday for the next 3 months.

B. Family

How will your household be shaped by this vision?

Example from the handout: Spend 30 minutes each evening praying and reading Scripture with my family.

C. Finance

What will you set aside for God's work?

Example from the handout: Save 10% of my income each month to build an emergency fund for my family.

D. Health

The body is part of the stewardship, too.

Example from the handout: Walk for 30 minutes, four times a week, for the next 6 weeks.

E. Social life

Who will you build deeper friendship with?

Example from the handout: Host a monthly dinner with close friends to nurture deeper relationships.

F. Intellectual growth

What will you learn in order to serve better?

Example from the handout: Read one Christian leadership book every month for the next 6 months.

G. Spiritual life

The root of everything above.

Example from the handout: Spend 20 minutes in prayer and Bible reading every morning for the next 3 months.
