Every note is stored on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, and no one else can see it. Clearing the wall deletes the notes permanently.

How the wall works

  • One concern at a timeThe handout’s instruction: each person writes a single concern, and the group prays through them all.
  • Then pray it aloudA note that never leaves the screen is a list. Read it back to God.
  • Mark the answersGive thanks when a note is answered. Remembering is part of the praying.

Need a starting point?

The practice comes from Key Characteristics of Gen Z. Put your goals beside it in the goal planner.
